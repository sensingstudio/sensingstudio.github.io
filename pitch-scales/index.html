Pitch & Loudness Scales

Two staircases of tones span the same range. One climbs in equal linear steps; the other climbs in equal ratios. On paper the linear one is the “even” one — but play them and the logarithmic staircase is the one that sounds evenly spaced, because the ear judges both pitch and loudness by ratio.
