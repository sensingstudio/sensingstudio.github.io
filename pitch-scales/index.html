Linear vs Log Pitch

Two staircases of tones span the same range. One climbs in equal steps of hertz; the other climbs in equal ratios. On paper the linear one is the “even” one — but play them and the logarithmic staircase is the one that sounds evenly spaced, because the ear hears pitch by ratio.
